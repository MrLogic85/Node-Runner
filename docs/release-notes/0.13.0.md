The first public build of Node Runner: draw a creature, watch it learn to move, and see why.

Node Runner is a learn-by-playing game about neural networks. You build a 2D creature from joints, beams and parts, and it gets a small brain: its senses are the inputs, its moving parts the outputs. Then you watch a crowd of shadows try, generation after generation, until the brain works out how to move.

> **Pre-alpha.** Things will change a lot between versions, and your creations may not survive an update.

## Install

1. Download `node-runner-0.13.0.apk` below on a 64-bit Android phone or tablet.
2. Open it and allow installing from this source when Android asks.

## Getting started

### Creations
The home screen lists your creations. Each card shows how far, how fast and how high the creature got in its latest training, and how many generations it has trained. Tap a card to open it, or use Copy and Delete on the card.

- **+ New** starts an empty creation.
- The first time you start, a **Walker** is already waiting: two crossed pistons swing two legs. Tap it, then Play, to watch it learn to walk.
- **⋯ → Examples** gives you a fresh copy of the Walker whenever you want one.

### Build
Everything you change is saved as you go, and Undo and Redo are at the top. Pinch to zoom and drag with two fingers to look around. The rail on the left has four tools:

- **Parts:** drag an Accelerometer from the tray onto a beam. It senses how the beam moves. Motors, the Camera and power parts are shown locked; they are coming later.
- **Beams:** drag from joint to joint to draw a Beam, or pick a Piston or a Spring from the list first. A Piston pushes and pulls and is what the brain moves; a Spring gives way and springs back.
- **Joint:** tap empty space to add a joint.
- **Select:** tap a part, or drag a box around several, to see its settings. A selected group can be moved, turned and resized with its handles.

When the creature is ready, tap **Play** at the bottom of the rail. If something is missing, Build tells you what.

### Train
Choose how many shadows train at once (up to 100) and how long each run lasts, then tap **Start**.

- Each shadow is a slightly different version of the brain. They all run at once, and the best of each generation leads the next.
- The camera follows one creature and zooms to keep it in view. Tap a shadow in the strip below the arena to follow it instead.
- The Start sign marks where runs begin, the Best flag the farthest any run has got, and the ruler shows metres.
- Tap the **Brain** button at the top, or Brain in the Status panel, to see which sense pushes which output, and how strongly.
- Training saves after every generation. Go back whenever you like; next time it carries on where it stopped.

### After training
- A trained creation **locks** so it isn't changed by accident. Tap the padlock at the top of Build to unlock it and keep building: what the brain has learned stays, and new parts join in gently. It locks again the next time you open it.
- **Simulate** in Train setup lets you watch what the creature has learned without training it further.
- In Build, **⋯ → Reset training** starts it over, after asking first.
