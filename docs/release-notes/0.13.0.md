The first public build of Node Runner: draw a creature, watch it learn to move, and pick its training up again later.

## What's new
- Build a creature from joints and beams, then add Pistons and Springs between joints and an Accelerometer on a beam.
- Select several parts at once to move, turn or resize them together, undo and redo any change, and pinch to zoom.
- Start from the Walker example, or copy any creation to try a variation. Every change is saved as you go.
- Train with up to 100 shadows trying at once. Tap a shadow in the strip below the arena to follow it, and see the best distance so far and where the run started.
- The camera follows the creature you watch and zooms to keep it in view, and distances are shown in metres.
- Training saves after every generation. Leave, come back later, and it carries on where it stopped.
- Once a creation is trained, Simulate lets you watch what it has learned without training it further.
- A trained creation locks so it isn't changed by accident. Unlock it to keep building: what it has learned stays, and new parts join in gently.
- Reset training starts a creation over, after asking first.
- Creation cards show how far, how fast and how high the creature got, and how many generations it has trained.
- Lines and icons stay sharp on any screen, and the app has its own launcher icon.
