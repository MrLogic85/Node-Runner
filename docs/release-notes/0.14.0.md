Give your creatures muscles in their joints and eyes on the ground, then send your best builds to a friend.

> **Alpha.** Your creations and their training from 0.13.0 come along. Pistons and Springs changed a little, so a trained creature may move slightly differently.

## Install

1. Download `node-runner-0.14.0.apk` below on a 64-bit Android phone or tablet.
2. Open it and allow installing from this source when Android asks. It installs over 0.13.0 and keeps your creations.

### Knees and hips that bend

Until now, only Pistons could move a creature. Now you can put a **Servo** on a joint, and the brain bends it like a knee or a hip. You choose how far it turns, where it starts, and how strong and fast it is.

![A creature with a Servo in each knee and a Camera fanning five rays over the ground](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/587cf48d-59cf-442a-b7d0-fc1df815ddc2)

### Eyes on the ground

A **Camera** on a beam looks at the ground along a fan of one, three or five rays. Aim it, then set how wide and how far it looks. Each ray tells the brain how near the ground is, so it knows when it is tipping or dropping before it lands.

### Building goes faster

Tap a part in the tray and Build lights up every place it fits. Then tap one to put it there. Built one good leg? Copy it and you have the second. Draw a Piston or a Spring over a beam to swap it. You can also move a sensor to another beam, and the brain keeps what it learned from it.

![Build lights up every place a part fits](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/669fbda7-5a4a-4a84-b3ee-784499a69d3f)

### Keep building after training

A trained creature no longer has to be unlocked for small changes. You can add and remove joints, beams and Springs right away. If something is still in the way, Build tells you which part.

### Share your creature

**Share build** in Build's **⋯** menu turns your creature into a short code you can paste in a chat. Your friend pastes it into **Import creation** on the home screen. They get the build without your training, so their creature learns with a brain of its own.

![Import shows a friend's creature and what it is built from before you add it](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d72c537e-33ff-4605-8131-5df722cb01de)

### See how the run is going

Next to the generation, a bar now fills up as the current run goes on, so you can see how long it has left. Training also warns you when too many shadows slow your phone down.

![A creature with Servos and a Camera training, with the run's bar filling up next to the generation](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d58def49-e9d6-40b7-8598-241230bc42f2)
