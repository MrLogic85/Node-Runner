Two new parts, the Servo and the Camera, more ways to build, and share codes to send your builds to friends.

> **Alpha.** Your creations and their training from 0.13.0 carry over. Pistons and Springs changed a little, so a trained creature may move slightly differently than before.

## Install

1. Download `node-runner-0.14.0.apk` below on a 64-bit Android phone or tablet.
2. Open it and allow installing from this source when Android asks. It installs over 0.13.0 and keeps your creations.

## What's new
- **Servo:** a motor on a joint. It holds one link and turns another, and the brain decides how far. Set its range, start position, strength, speed and rise time.
- **Camera:** a sensor on a beam that looks for the ground along a fan of rays. Drag its round handle to aim it, and choose how many rays, how wide they fan out and how far they reach. It also tells the brain whether any ray sees the ground at all.
- **Piston:** Stroke now sets how much of its length it travels, up to all of it, and the new Start position sets where it starts.
- **Spring:** set its Stroke and Coil length, and see the coil drawn in detail.
- **Share build:** copy a creation's build as a short code from Build's ⋯ menu and paste it in a chat. **Import creation** in Creations' ⋯ menu adds it back. The training stays with you, so the copy learns from scratch.
- **Copy** several selected parts at once, Pistons, sensors and Servos included. On a locked creation, Copy takes joints, beams and Springs.
- **Placing parts:** tap a part in the tray, then tap where it goes. Build shows where it fits.
- **Links:** the Beams tool is now called Links. While you drag a link, the line shows whether it will attach, and a refused Beam says why. Draw a Piston or Spring between two joints a beam already joins, and it replaces the beam.
- **Move a sensor** to another beam; the brain keeps what it learned from it.
- **Locked creations:** you can now add and delete joints, beams and Springs without unlocking. When something is locked, Build says why and which part is in the way.
- **Part settings** explain themselves while you touch a slider, and less-used ones sit under Advanced.
- **Training** shows how far the current run has got, warns when many shadows may run slowly, and a tapped shadow is followed only until the next generation.
- The Brain view names Camera and Piston inputs and outputs more clearly.
- Locked parts in the tray say which version brings them.
- Names are capped in length and start with a capital letter on the keyboard.

## Fixed
- The Training camera no longer stutters while it follows and zooms.
- A creature in separate, unjoined pieces can no longer start training.
- Undo of a delete selects the restored parts again.
- The Best flag looks as designed and is no longer cut off at the right edge.
- The Brain view no longer squashes its labels when a creature has many senses.
- The Piston's shortest stroke mark is no longer hidden behind its cylinder.
- The launch screen on Android 12 and later is dark, like the app.
