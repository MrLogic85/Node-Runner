Give your creatures muscles in their joints and eyes on the ground, then send your best builds to a friend.

> **Alpha.** Your creations and their training from 0.13.0 come along. Pistons and Springs changed a little, so a trained creature may move slightly differently.

## Install

1. Download `node-runner-0.14.0.apk` below on a 64-bit Android phone or tablet.
2. Open it and allow installing from this source when Android asks. It installs over 0.13.0 and keeps your creations.

## What's new

### Build creatures that bend, look and bounce
- **Put a Servo on a joint** and let the brain bend it, like a knee or a hip. Choose how far it may turn, where it starts, and how strong and fast it is.
- **Give your creature eyes.** A Camera on a beam sees the ground along a fan of rays. Aim it, pick one, three or five rays, and set how wide and how far it looks. Now the brain can react to what lies ahead.
- **Tune your Pistons and Springs.** Let a Piston travel its whole length and choose where it starts. Give a Spring its own stroke and coil length, and see its coil drawn in detail.

### Build faster
- **Tap a part, then tap where it goes.** Build lights up every place it fits.
- **Copy a group of parts** to build a second leg in one go. On a trained creature, Copy takes joints, beams and Springs.
- **Move a sensor to another beam** without losing what the brain learned from it.
- **Draw with Links**, the tool that used to be called Beams. The line shows whether it will attach before you let go, and a refused link says why.
- **Swap a beam for a Piston or Spring** by drawing it right over the beam.
- **Keep building a trained creature.** Add and remove joints, beams and Springs without unlocking it. When something is locked, Build tells you which part is in the way.
- **Touch a slider to learn what it does.** Rarely used settings wait under Advanced.

### Share
- **Send a build to a friend** as a short code they paste into Import. They get your creature and train their own brain for it.

### Train
- **See how far the current run has got**, and get a warning before too many shadows slow your phone down.
- **Follow any shadow you tap** until the next generation starts.
- **Read the brain more easily.** Every Camera ray and Piston output has a clear name.

### And also
- Parts still to come say which version brings them.
- Names start with a capital letter, and are kept short enough to fit.

## Fixed
- The Training camera no longer stutters while it follows and zooms.
- A creature in separate pieces can no longer start training.
- Undo of a delete selects the parts it brings back.
- The Best flag is no longer cut off at the right edge.
- The Piston's shortest stroke mark is no longer hidden behind its cylinder.
- The Brain view keeps its labels readable with many senses.
- The launch screen on Android 12 and later is dark, like the app.
