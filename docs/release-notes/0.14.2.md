Give your creatures motors that spin, brakes that hold, a feel for their own joints, and Pistons that reach further.

> **Alpha.** Your creations and their training from 0.14.1 come along. The physics is a little stiffer, so a trained creature may move slightly differently. Pistons and Springs need a little more room, so one squeezed between two Servos may need its joints moved apart before it trains again.

## Install

1. Download `node-runner-0.14.2.apk` below on a 64-bit Android phone or tablet.
2. Open it and allow installing from this source when Android asks. It installs over 0.14.1 and keeps your creations.

### Motors that spin

Put a **Motor** on a joint and the brain chooses how fast it turns and how hard it pushes, round and round if it likes. Turn a Wheel with it and your creature can drive.

![A small car training with a Motor on each wheel, the front one tapped to show what goes in to the brain and what comes out](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e4362df4-bf98-4821-ad79-615d67b8cd20)

### Brakes that hold

A **Brake** on a joint stops its two sides turning against each other, up to the strength you set, or locks them together. Lock a crank arm to a Wheel and a Piston can drive it like a steam train. A Brake needs no brain, so you can add one to a trained creature too.

![Build with a train's middle wheel selected, its Brake set to Locked with the Wheel as Fixed and the crank arm as Target](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e035e40d-6449-4dce-8855-071c7459b2d3)

### Feel how a joint bends

An **Angle sensor** on a joint tells the brain the angle between two of its sides and how fast it changes, so your creature can feel how bent a knee is, or where its crank is in a turn.

![A train rolling as its Piston drives the wheels, with its Angle sensor tapped and the angle bars moving as the crank turns](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cc518aef-4817-4cdc-a076-6ebea4b47ff1)

### Pistons that reach further

Pistons and Springs now slide out in three parts, so their **Stroke** goes up to 200%, twice as far as before.

![Build with a Piston selected and its Stroke set to 200%](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/76b5bee6-06d2-41ad-ad8e-5366323e99a3)

## Fixed

Large creatures keep their shape: their beams no longer stretch out of shape and their triangles no longer flip inside out under load.
