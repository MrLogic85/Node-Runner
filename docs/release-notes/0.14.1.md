Put your creatures on wheels, give their feet grip and a sense of touch, and watch the brain at work while they move.

> **Alpha.** Your creations and their training from 0.14.0 come along. The physics changed a little, so a trained creature may move slightly differently.

## Install

1. Download `node-runner-0.14.1.apk` below on a 64-bit Android phone or tablet.
2. Open it and allow installing from this source when Android asks. It installs over 0.14.0 and keeps your creations.

### Roll on wheels

Put a **Wheel** on a joint and your creature can roll instead of walk. A Wheel turns freely, so something has to push: legs, a Piston, or a Servo on the Wheel's own joint that turns it back and forth. A Wheel needs no brain, so you can add one to a trained creature too.

![A creature rolling on one big wheel, its toe touching the ground behind it](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bfce21ca-0e70-434b-80a2-af26e0fe0b3a)

### Grip and touch

**Rubber** on a joint makes it, or its Wheel's tyre, grip the ground twice as hard, so it slips less. A **Touch sensor** on a joint tells the brain how close that joint is to the ground, so it can feel each step land.

### Several parts on one joint

One joint can now hold a Wheel, a Servo, a Rubber and a Touch sensor at once. In Build, the **On this joint** tabs switch between them.

![Build with a Servo selected on a wheel's joint, its tabs showing the Servo, the Wheel and a Touch sensor, and the Wheel as the Servo's Target](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a2bdbd35-050a-465b-9cc3-281e7246ec8b)

### Watch the brain at work

Tap a part while your creature trains or runs, and live bars show what goes **IN** to the brain and what comes **OUT**. Tap a joint to see all of its parts at once.

![The same creature with its wheel's joint tapped, the card showing the Servo, the Wheel and the Touch sensor with live IN and OUT bars](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0a76b18c-1207-4b1b-822a-4665a630af21)
