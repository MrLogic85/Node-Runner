Pick a light or dark look, build on a grid that snaps, and watch your creature's senses and drives move live as it trains.

> **Alpha.** Your creations and their training from 0.14.3 come along. Each one opens in Build on the 0.5 m grid with Snap to grid off, as before.

## Install

1. Download `node-runner-0.14.4.apk` below on a 64-bit Android phone or tablet.
2. Open it and allow installing from this source when Android asks. It installs over 0.14.3 and keeps your creations.

### Neon, Paper or your phone's mode

Open **Settings** from the ⋮ menu on Creations and pick the dark **Neon** look, the light **Paper** look, or **Use phone** to follow your phone's dark or light mode. The whole app changes at once, the Training world included.

![Settings in Paper, with Neon, Paper and Use phone in the Theme row above the Grid default](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/26633d3d-f25d-47ee-a60e-214c145c4860)

### Build on a grid

Tap the grid button in Build's toolbar to choose a 0.25, 0.5 or 1 m grid and turn on **Snap to grid**, so the joints you place and move land on it. Each creation keeps its own grid, and Settings sets the one a new creation starts with.

![The Walker in Build in Neon, with the grid menu open on 0.25 m and Snap to grid on](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e40eaafc-2dd3-477c-904b-ca216ac2630f)

### A Status panel that moves with the creature

While a creature trains, the Status panel shows a live bar for each of its senses and a dial for each part it drives. The Distance card adds the current speed and a strip showing how close the run is to the best so far, and the Network card says how many numbers training is tuning.

![The Walker training, with live input bars, output dials, 28 parameters and the Distance card at 6.0 m of a 6.5 m best](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2c3edddf-af65-44ad-ab56-ab7ab197c36d)
