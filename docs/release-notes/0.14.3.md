Give your creatures Steppers that tick like a clock, and watch weak builds break apart.

> **Alpha.** Your creations and their training from 0.14.2 come along. A Locked Brake now holds more steadily, so a trained creature with one may move a little differently. A creature that pulled its joints apart before may now break, and its distance stops counting where it broke.

## Install

1. Download `node-runner-0.14.3.apk` below on a 64-bit Android phone or tablet.
2. Open it and allow installing from this source when Android asks. It installs over 0.14.2 and keeps your creations.

### Steppers that tick

A **Stepper** holds its joint in fixed notches, like the hand of a clock. The network chooses how often it ticks on to the next notch and which way, and can feel each tick coming, so it can time the rest of the body to it.

![A creature training with two Steppers, one tapped to show the angle and tick going in to the network and the frequency coming out](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/430d6f1c-9a17-4069-99c4-c850e81b2231)

### Beams that tear

Build a creature too weak for how it moves, and its beams pull off their joints. A red pulse marks the joint that gave way, loose parts fall to the ground, and a **Crash** flag shows where it broke; nothing after that counts.

![A long bridge breaking apart in Training, its legs and wheels torn off and a Crash flag at 3.6 m](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bb21849c-e0b9-4a46-afe0-b2309fadad7a)
