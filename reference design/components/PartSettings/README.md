The **settings panel** for one selected part. It replaces the tool's panel in the **side panel** (see ComponentToolbars), in any tool. The panel's own row carries the part's glyph and its **kind** ("Accelerometer", "Joint"), so a renamed part still says what it is; there is no close button, and tapping empty canvas deselects.

**Rows, in order.** **Name** (a default such as "Joint 2", "Beam 1", "Accel 1" or "Servo 1" until the player names it; at most 10 characters), what the part is joined to where the canvas does not show it (a beam's two joints, "Between"; a sensor's beam, "On"), the **basic sliders**, a Servo's **Fixed link** and **Target link** pickers, the **Advanced** section, the part's **note** (one line: what it does), then a full-width `danger` **Delete**. Delete acts on a tap, with no dialog; Undo brings the part back, selected again. On a locked creation Delete shows locked when the deletion would change the brain, and a tap says why.

**Basic and Advanced.** Each setting is declared basic or advanced. Basic sliders are always shown. Advanced ones sit in an **Advanced** expand section, closed every time Build opens and kept open or closed while the selection changes; a part with no advanced settings has no section. Advanced: Start position, Max speed, Rise time, Damping and Coil length.

- **Joint**: Name and the note "Links meet and turn here. Drag it to move them." No settings.
- **Beam**: Name, Between, "A rigid rod."
- **Piston**: Max strength (20–400 N) and Stroke (10–100% of its gap); Advanced: Start position (0–100%), Max speed (0.5–4 m/s) and Rise time (0.1, 0.2, 0.5 or 1 s, evenly spaced). "Extends and retracts."
- **Spring**: Stiffness (50–2000 N/m) and Stroke; Advanced: Damping (0–100 N·s/m) and Coil length (0–100%). "Extends and retracts toward its built length."
- **Servo**: Max strength (5–200 N·m) and Range (20–360°), then the pickers; Advanced: Start position, Max speed (30–720°/s) and Rise time. "A motor that tries to hold a target angle."
- **Accelerometer** and **Camera**: Name, On, the note; no sliders yet. A Camera selected alone shows its rays and an Aim handle on the canvas; its note adds "Drag the round handle to aim it."

A slider is the maximum or the range the brain works within: Max strength is the most the part can use, and the brain picks how much of it from moment to moment. The canvas shows what a slider changes while it can be changed: a Piston's or Spring's stops as ticks, a Spring's rest length as a ring. Sliders show no step labels. A finger on a slider shows what it does in a box at the canvas's top right (see Build). Several selected parts share the sliders they all have.

**The Servo's pickers.** The list holds every link at the Servo's joint (Beam, Piston or Spring); picking the link the other role has swaps the two. The accessory is the beam icon, in `detail` for Fixed (the colour of its hatched band on the canvas) and `accent` for Target. A missing role reads "Pick a Fixed link" or "Pick a Target link" in `danger`; on a joint with fewer than two links the note says "A Servo needs two links at its joint". On a trained creation each picker has the note "Changing these makes it learn again." under it, and on a locked creation the pickers are disabled.

**Planned parts** keep the designs they had until they are built: Brake, Stepper, Velocity motor, Wheel and a motor on a wheel, Wing, and the blocks. Their pickers are named Fixed link and Target link like the Servo's; their Power and Weighs rows wait for power (0.18) and may change.
