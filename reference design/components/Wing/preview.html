Start, end and normal
The normal is start to end turned a quarter turn. That side is the lift side. Forces act at the centre.
Gliding
Moving along the beam in either direction gives the same lift. Only the speed along the beam counts.
Flapping
Moving down against the normal gives lift. Moving up along it gives none, so a flap is a power stroke.
Flip
Flip swaps start and end, so the normal, and the lift side, turns over. The beam itself does not move.
Placing it
Pick Wing in the Links tab, then drag from one node to another, like the Beam tool. It is a beam with a lift side.
