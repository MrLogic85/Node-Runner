More from the [Component Library](../ComponentLibrary/README.md): frames, a card's action bar, an overflow menu, a picker, a part in the tray, a part's settings panel and the stage card.

- **Picker.** A row with an optional small accessory (an icon, or a caller-drawn swatch: the picker does not know which), the current choice, and a chevron. Opened, it lists the choices under it in the compact Menu, set to mark its choice (`c_menu(..., compact=True, marks=True)`), each row with the same kind of accessory in the icon slot; the chosen one is washed and checked, and a choice can carry a short note under its name ("swaps", on Parts, for a choice that needs explaining rather than being refused). **Locked** shows a lock instead of a chevron and tints the row in accent: there is no other choice to make, ever. **Disabled** is the ordinary disabled state every other control has: the whole row dims to 50% with a dashed border, the chevron stays (it still works, it just can't be opened right now) — a line nearby says why. The two never combine.
- **Menu.** One component, `c_menu`, in the menu frame (with its glow), two sizes that share everything but the row: **Standard** is the overflow list, rows at least `touch` (48) high with `body-strong` labels and `icon-lg` icons; **Compact** is the list a picker opens, rows at least `control-sm` (32) with `small-strong` labels and `icon` icons. **Width**: fixed, 200 by default, or fit to its content, never narrower than what it hangs from (a picker's list). Rows, in either size:
  - **Action row** (`mi`): one slot first, an icon or an accessory (a picker's swatch; never both), then the label, with an optional **note** under it as a second line (`note`, `muted`), and, only in a menu set to mark its choice, an `icon-sm` check in `accent` at the end of the chosen row. All centred vertically. The **chosen** row has an `accent-soft` wash; a **pressed** row gets the same wash. **Danger**: label and icon in `danger` (a note is still allowed). **Disabled**: label and icon in `muted`.
  - **Toggle row** (`mi_toggle`): the library's toggle row as it is, label, optional help line and switch, inside the menu's side padding and at least its row height. Tapping it flips the setting and leaves the menu open.
  - **Divider** (`MI_DIVIDER`): a `line` hairline put in by hand to group rows, never drawn between every row. It is inset by the row's side padding, with `space-2` above and below it in Standard and `space-1` in Compact. In both, the current or chosen row has an `accent-soft` wash.
- **Card.** One frame at rest (`edge` 1px, `radius-lg`, `panel`, `space-3` padding) with variants: `sel` (accent 2px and its own `glow` shadow), `lock` (accent, dashed -- no other choice, ever, the same idea as `c_pick(label, value, accessory, open, opts, lock, dis)`'s `lock`), `warn` (`danger` border, always with words), `hint` and `raised`, and the padding sizes `snug`, `tight`, `roomy` and `flush`. Any of them can also glow: a separate on/off, `c_card(..., glow=True)`, box-shadow in that frame's own border colour (`edge-glow` for the plain panel, `accent-glow` for `sel` and `lock`, `danger-glow` for `warn`, `halo-glow` for `hint`, `line-strong-glow` for `raised`) -- never a fixed colour, and never a kind of its own. A third, independent on/off, `disabled`, dims any of them with a dashed `line-strong` border and no glow -- for something temporarily unavailable, the same idea as `c_pick(label, value, accessory, open, opts, lock, dis)`'s `dis` and `c_slider`'s `enabled=False`; a frame that is locked and also unavailable right now is both, `c_card(..., kind='lock', disabled=True)`. Menu, dialog, notification and the chosen stage card are the frames `menu`, `dialog`, `toast` and `pick`, not picked freely; a dialog or notification takes its severity as a variant (`hint` for warn, `warn` for danger), which colours both its border and its glow. Cards on Creations, Achievements and Train setup, the settings panel and dialogs are all this frame.
- **Card actions.** `c_card_actions`: the actions along the bottom of a flush card (a creation on Creations, a checkpoint on Checkpoints), always visible, never behind a menu. It is a layout style for buttons, not a button of its own: each action is an ordinary stacked button (`flat`, or `tertiary` for a destructive one, which puts it in the danger colour) and keeps everything a button does. The bar only restyles them: each stretches to its share of the width and `touch` high -- that whole cell is what you tap -- and drops its frame and background. A hairline separates the cells and runs above the row. The card keeps the bar's height (`touch`) free at its bottom.
- **List row.** A part in the tray: a glyph, a name and how many are left, `control` high with a `radius-md` border. States: at rest, selected (`accent`), locked (dashed with a lock) and none left (dimmed).
- **Panel.** `c_inspector` is a part's settings panel: `c_panel_head` at the top (an optional glyph, the title in `subheading` — it truncates with an ellipsis, never wraps — and icon actions on the right, each `control-sm` wide, the same header any side panel uses), then `c_rows`. Every row inside is built with `pad=False`: it carries no margin of its own, whatever kind of row it is, and `c_rows` alone sets the gap between them (`space-1`), the same regardless of which two row kinds happen to be next to each other. Deleting a part is `c_rows`'s own full-width `danger` button at the end, with a bigger gap above it (`space-2`): an action, not another row.
- **Stage card.** `c_stage`: a numbered card of the signal-flow column, `c_num` leading its header, the stage name in `stage`, an optional right-aligned `note`, then whatever that stage shows. `collapsed` is the compact strip used when only one stage is expanded at a time; `sel` is the accent ring a tapped stage gets.
