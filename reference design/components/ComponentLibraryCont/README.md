More from the [Component Library](../ComponentLibrary/README.md): frames, an overflow menu, a picker, a part in the tray, a part's settings panel and the stage card.

- **Picker.** A row with an optional small accessory (an icon, or a caller-drawn swatch: the picker does not know which), the current choice, and a chevron. Opened, it lists the choices under it in the same frame as Menu (`c_card` kind `menu`), each row with the same kind of accessory; a choice can carry a check (selected) or a short note instead of a check ("swaps", on Parts, for a choice that needs explaining rather than being refused). **Locked** shows a lock instead of a chevron and tints the row in accent: there is no other choice to make, ever. **Disabled** is the ordinary disabled state every other control has: the whole row dims to 50% with a dashed border, the chevron stays (it still works, it just can't be opened right now) — a line nearby says why. The two never combine.
- **Menu.** The overflow list: 48px rows with an icon, a word, and `danger` for destructive ones.
- **Card.** One frame at rest (`edge` 1px, `radius-lg`, `panel`, `space-3` padding) with variants: `sel` (accent 2px and its own `glow` shadow), `lock` (accent, dashed -- no other choice, ever, the same idea as `c_pick(label, value, accessory, open, opts, lock, dis)`'s `lock`), `warn` (`danger` border, always with words), `hint` and `raised`, and the padding sizes `snug`, `tight`, `roomy` and `flush`. Any of them can also glow: a separate on/off, `c_card(..., glow=True)`, box-shadow in that frame's own border colour (`edge-glow` for the plain panel, `accent-glow` for `sel` and `lock`, `danger-glow` for `warn`, `halo-glow` for `hint`, `line-strong-glow` for `raised`) -- never a fixed colour, and never a kind of its own. A third, independent on/off, `disabled`, dims any of them with a dashed `line-strong` border and no glow -- for something temporarily unavailable, the same idea as `c_pick(label, value, accessory, open, opts, lock, dis)`'s `dis` and `c_slider`'s `enabled=False`; a frame that is locked and also unavailable right now is both, `c_card(..., kind='lock', disabled=True)`. Menu, dialog and the chosen stage card are the frames `menu`, `dialog` and `pick`, not picked freely. Cards on Creations, Achievements and Train setup, the settings panel and dialogs are all this frame.
- **List row.** A part in the tray: a glyph, a name and how many are left, `control` high with a `radius-md` border. States: at rest, selected (`accent`), locked (dashed with a lock) and none left (dimmed).
- **Panel.** `c_inspector` is a part's settings panel: `c_panel_head` at the top (an optional glyph, the title in `subheading` — it truncates with an ellipsis, never wraps — and icon actions on the right, each `control-sm` wide, the same header any side panel uses), then `c_rows`. Every row inside is built with `pad=False`: it carries no margin of its own, whatever kind of row it is, and `c_rows` alone sets the gap between them (`space-1`), the same regardless of which two row kinds happen to be next to each other. Deleting a part is `c_rows`'s own full-width `danger` button at the end, with a bigger gap above it (`space-2`): an action, not another row.
- **Stage card.** `c_stage`: a numbered card of the signal-flow column, `c_num` leading its header, the stage name in `stage`, an optional right-aligned `note`, then whatever that stage shows. `collapsed` is the compact strip used when only one stage is expanded at a time; `sel` is the accent ring a tapped stage gets.
