The words the product teaches, each with one glyph that never changes between screens.

- **Beam**: a `line-strong` rod, `stroke-beam` (3px), round caps, between two nodes. Beams are drawn, and there is no limit.
- **Parts** are dragged in from the Parts tray and are limited: **Node** (a ring in `panel` with a `line-strong` outline, filled `accent` while it fires), **Core** (a node wrapped in an `accent` diamond; a sensor, the **eyes, never the brain**), **Motor** (an `accent` arc with an arrowhead placed on a hinge, `stroke-signal` 2px; it lets the brain twist that joint) and **Spring** (a zigzag beam that pulls back on its own; unlocked by an achievement).

Rigid rule: three beams closing a triangle are hatched (`muted`, 60%) and have no joint, so a motor cannot be dropped there. Two beams sharing a node make a hinge, and dropping a motor on it enables the brain to twist it. The hatch carries the meaning in grayscale.

The causal chain is the sentence every screen serves: cores read the world, the brain decides, motors twist the body, the body moves, distance is the score. The numbered circles 1 to 4 in the signal-flow column carry it.
