Build is only for a new, unsaved creation. Nothing else in the app adds or removes parts.

**Three tools on the 56px left rail.**

- **Move** is the default. Drag a node or part to move it; **tap one part or beam to select it** (its settings open on the right), tap empty canvas to deselect.
- **Beam** draws beams, unlimited: drag from a node to a node or to empty space.
- **Select** is the multi-select tool: drag a box or tap several parts (dashed `halo` box, ringed parts). Drag any selected part to move them all, or **Delete** them at once.

There is no Delete tool. A single part is deleted from its **settings panel** (trash icon in the header); several are deleted from the **selection panel**. Deleting a node also removes the beams on it.

**Everything else is a part you drag in** from the **Parts tray**: Node, Core, Motor and, once unlocked, Spring. You start with a limited number of each ("1 left"); "0 left" is dimmed and cannot be picked up; an achievement-locked part is dashed with a lock. Deleting a part gives it back to the count.

**One right-hand panel, three states.** Nothing selected: the Parts tray. One part selected: its settings (see PartSettings). Several selected: the selection panel. Only one is ever visible. The tray also collapses to a 28px handle.

**Brain chip.** Next to Save, a chip shows the brain's shape ("Brain 1 × 4") and opens **Brain setup** (see BrainSetup): hidden layers 1 to 3 and neurons per layer 1 to 100.

**Title and Save.** The top bar holds the editable name and **Save**, disabled while anything is invalid (the tray says why, in `danger`). Saving opens the creation (see Creation).
