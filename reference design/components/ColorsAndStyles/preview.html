Coloursevery colour is one of these tokens, defined once per theme and used by name; nothing else
Neon lab (dark)
bg
App background and the arena. Text on it: ink, muted, accent.
panel
Top bar, side panels, stage cards, sheets. Text on it: ink, muted, accent.
panel-raised
Buttons, fields, segmented controls, cards sitting on bg or panel. Text on it: ink, muted.
line
Decorative hairlines, grid dots, track of a progress bar. Never the only cue for a control.
line-strong
Control borders, beams, unselected node outlines, empty-slot outlines. At least 3:1 on bg, panel and panel-raised in both themes.
ink
Primary text, node outlines, readout values on bg, panel and panel-raised.
muted
Labels, units, captions, disabled explanations on bg, panel and panel-raised (4.5:1 or better).
accent
The one signal colour: primary action, live signal, firing neurons, motor arcs, cores, focus ring, progress fill. Reads on bg, panel and panel-raised.
edge
Outline of the frame, top bar, side panels and cards: a cyan-tinted hairline in neon, a neutral warm grey in paper. Decorative; controls use line-strong.
on-accent
Text and icons on accent fills.
halo
Selection and focus of a thing the player tapped: ring around a part, outline of a selected stage. Static, never pulses. Always with a name label.
danger
Invalid anatomy and destructive actions, always with a warn icon and a plain sentence. Reads on bg and panel.
scrim
Dims the screen behind a dialog or sheet. Decorative; the dialog itself carries the message.
output
Outputs: motors and the neurons that drive them, when a part is inspected. Senses use accent. Never alone: always with a tag or a position.
