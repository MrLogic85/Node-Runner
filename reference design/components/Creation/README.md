Opened by **Edit** on a card in the Creations menu, or by saving a new build. It is the Build canvas with the **structure locked so the machine-learning model stays valid**.

**Rail.** **Move** (drag; tap one part to select it) and **Select** (drag a box to move several parts together) work. **Beam** is dimmed, dashed and locked. There is no Delete anywhere, and the settings and selection panels have no trash. A chip says "Parts locked · drag to move". A moved part is ringed in `halo` and its old place stays as a dashed ghost.

**One right-hand panel, three states.** Nothing selected: how trained it is (generations, best distance) and three actions: **Resume training** (the one primary, opens Train setup), **Stats** and **Brain**. One part selected: its settings (name, weight, strength and so on). Several selected: a selection panel that says they can only be moved. Never two at once. Settings that change structure (beam length, rest length) show a lock.

**Top bar.** Back, the creation name editable in place (pencil), and overflow (Reset training, Delete creation).
