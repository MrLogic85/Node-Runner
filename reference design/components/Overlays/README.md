Sheets and toasts over a dimmed screen, one per risky or notable moment. There is no rebuild or duplicate dialog: parts are locked after saving, and Copy makes an identical creation without asking.

**Reset training** (in the Creation overflow) names what is lost and needs a **press-and-hold** on a `danger` button (a fill sweeps across, about 0.8 s). **Delete** asks once. Both are followed by an **Undo toast** with a visible 10 s countdown. **Copy** shows a toast with **Open**. **Unlocks** (a new part or a new map) are a glowing `accent` card with the reward glyph, the name and the achievement that earned it; shown once, never blocking controls, and they link to Achievements.

Buttons are 48px and named with verbs. Never place two destructive actions side by side.
