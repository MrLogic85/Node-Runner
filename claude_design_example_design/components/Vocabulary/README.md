The four words the whole product teaches, each with one glyph that never changes between screens:

- **Node**: a ring in `panel` with a `line-strong` outline, r 6; filled `accent` while it fires.
- **Beam**: a `line-strong` rod, `stroke-beam` (3px), round caps, between two nodes.
- **Core**: a node wrapped in an `accent` diamond, plus a small centre dot when drawn large. It is the **eyes, not the brain**: never draw a core with the brain glyph, never call it "brain" in copy.
- **Motor relation**: an `accent` arc with an arrowhead at a node where two beams meet, `stroke-signal` (2px). It appears by itself.

Rigid rule: three beams closing a triangle are filled with a diagonal hatch (`muted`, 60%) and get no arcs; the hatch also carries the meaning in grayscale. Show this in Build the moment a triangle closes.

The causal chain below is the sentence every screen must serve: cores read the world, the brain decides, motors twist the body, the body moves, distance is the score. Use the numbered circles (1 to 4 in the signal-flow column) as its recurring markers.
