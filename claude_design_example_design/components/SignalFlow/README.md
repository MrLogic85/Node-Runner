The causal chain as four stacked cards joined by `accent` arrows: **1 Sees**, **2 Decides**, **3 Twists**, **4 Scores**. At rest each card shows only a picture: three level bars, a tiny network, two half-dials, the distance. There are no captions or numbers except the distance.

Tapping a body part **expands the matching card only** (named rows for Sees, a labelled dial for Twists) and outlines it in `halo`; the previous one collapses. Tapping Decides opens BrainFocus. Cards keep a fixed width (156px inside a 168px column) and expanded height grows by at most 32px, so nothing else moves.

If a creature has more than three cores or two motors, the collapsed card shows the first ones and the expanded card scrolls.
