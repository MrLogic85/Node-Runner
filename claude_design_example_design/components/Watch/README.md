The main screen. It shows four things at rest and reveals detail only on tap, so a phone held in landscape never feels crowded.

**Arena (left).** The creature, a ruler labelled every 2 m, and a dashed **best** marker. The camera follows, so the creature stays about 43% from the left edge and the ruler scrolls. Nothing else floats over the arena except a one-word label next to a part the player has tapped (a `halo` ring plus its name, "Left foot").

**Bottom row.** Pause, speed, and the generation strip with one line of text: "Generation 37 · try 5 of 8". This replaces the trial timer and all other progress text.

**Signal flow (right, 168px).** Four small cards joined by arrows: 1 Sees (three level bars), 2 Decides (a tiny network), 3 Twists (two dials), 4 Scores (distance). At rest a card has no labels or captions. Tapping a body part **expands only the matching card** to show named rows (here Foot L, Foot R, Tilt) with the tapped one outlined in `halo`; tapping another part collapses the first. Tapping the Decides card opens BrainFocus.

**Top bar.** Menu, creature name with a one-word status, the Watch / Build switch, overflow (Start over, Training settings, unlock progress). Progress to the next unlock is a 3px `accent` line along the bottom of the bar, no text; the number lives in the menu.

Rule of thumb for this screen: at most one sentence of text, one expanded card, one halo. All sizes assume the 640 x 360 canvas; scale the canvas, never reflow it.
