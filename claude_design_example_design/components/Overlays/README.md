Sheets and toasts, centred over a dimmed screen, one per risky or notable moment.

**Start over** is the only reset in the app (Randomize and Reset are merged). It names what is lost ("142 generations of training"), keeps the shape, and needs a **press-and-hold** on a `danger` button (a fill sweeps left to right, about 0.8 s) instead of a second tap, which suits a touch-only phone. Afterwards an **Undo toast** stays for 10 s with a visible countdown; Delete uses the same pattern.

**Rebuild** explains that a new shape means a new brain and defaults to keeping the old creature as a saved version ("Walker-1 (v1)"), so no path silently loses training. **Duplicate** asks whether to copy the brain. **Unlock** is a celebratory card in `accent` with the part name and who earned it; it appears once, over Simulate, and never blocks controls.

Buttons on a sheet are 48px, labelled with verbs ("Start fresh", "Copy brain"). Never place two destructive actions side by side.
